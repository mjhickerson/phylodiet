Tree: an open build from published chronograms (Smith & Brown 2018; Nitta et al. 2022; Rabosky et al. 2018; Upham et al. 2019; Jetz et al. 2012; Stein et al. 2018; Jetz & Pyron 2018; Tonini et al. 2016; Varga et al. 2019; Shen et al. 2020; Kim et al. 2026, red algal tree file courtesy of Hocheol Kim) on a Strassert et al. 2021 backbone, with skeleton nodes from the studies listed in the repository and five ages from TimeTree 5 (Kumar et al. 2022) by permission. Node ages reviewed by specialists are credited in the changelog.
